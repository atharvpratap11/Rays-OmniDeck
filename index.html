🚀

Integrated In-Browser Auto-Updates

Never reinstall manually. Rays OmniDeck silently checks GitHub Releases in the background, downloads delta patches, and presents a non-intrusive "Restart to Update" prompt when ready.

Why Rays OmniDeck?

Standard browsers leak cookies, shared sessions, and service workers across tabs. Rays OmniDeck solves concurrent multi-role testing at the architecture level.

🛡️

Strict Partition Isolation

Every tab operates in a distinct Chromium partition (persist:tab_<id>). Tab A (Doctor) and Tab B (Patient) store cookies, IndexedDB, and localStorage in separate databases with zero session bleeding.

🤖

Gemini AI QA Copilot

Slide-out AI copilot to generate synthetic patient profiles, realistic prescription payloads, multi-role test scenarios, and bug reports with one-click clipboard copying.

📱

Device Portviews & Resizer

Built-in viewport presets for iPhone 16 Pro Max, Galaxy S24 Ultra, Z Fold, and iPad Tablets with orientation rotation and interactive drag-to-stretch grab handles.

◫

Dual-Role Split View

Inspect two roles simultaneously side-by-side. Trigger a clinical action on the left and observe real-time webhook updates or pharmacy fulfillment on the right.

⚡

60 FPS & RAM Optimized

Engineered with hardware GPU rasterization, V8 memory boundary caps (512MB), and aggressive background tab throttling to ensure butter-smooth performance.

🍪

Per-Partition Cookie Inspector

Dedicated cookie inspector and DevTools targeting individual tab partitions. Reset a single user session with one click without disturbing other open tabs.
